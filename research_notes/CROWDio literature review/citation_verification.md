# Citation verification for [K]-tagged references in the CROWDio literature-review notes

## Are the [K] (memory-cited) references bibliographically correct?

### Takeaway
I checked all ~85 [K] or DOI-less references in web_backend_mcc_scheduling.md, web_mobile_inference.md (which has no [K] tags; I checked its unverified items), web_reconfiguration_planning.md and web_sdk_programming_models.md. I used Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=`, plus direct DOI lookups), OpenAlex (`api.openalex.org/works?search=` and the arXiv-DOI filter), and targeted web searches for USENIX-only papers. None of the references is fabricated. Every one exists. The errors are in author lists, a wrong DOI, and a wrong venue. The most important corrections are:
- **CWC:** the SDK note gives DOI 10.1145/2413176.2413204, which returns 404. The correct DOI is 10.1145/2413176.2413199. The backend note's "PerCom 2012" is also wrong: the paper is ACM CoNEXT 2012.
- **DADS:** the author list is wrong. It should be Hu, Bao, Wang, Liu.
- **JALAD:** the author list is wrong. It should be Li, Hu, Jiang, Wang, Wen, Zhu.
- **Parcae:** the author list is wrong.
- **DDPQN:** this does exist (Xue et al., IEEE TSC 2022). The notes' "not found" is wrong.
- **DeepFlow:** now retitled DeepServe on arXiv.
- **EDAS:** the year is 2015, not 2017.
- **Hinton:** the venue is the NIPS 2014 Deep Learning Workshop. The arXiv posting is 2015.

Status legend: **OK** means the citation as given in the notes is correct. **Corrected** means the work exists but some metadata in the notes was wrong or incomplete. **NOT FOUND** means no matching record (none in this set).

### Cited Findings (verification table)

Unless a row says otherwise, the sources are the Crossref record (`https://api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>`) and the OpenAlex record for the DOI shown. Rows confirmed only by web search link their source.
